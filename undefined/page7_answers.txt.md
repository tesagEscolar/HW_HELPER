The bar chart illustrates the housing choices for people aged 25 to 34 in the UK over a ten-year period from 2004 to 2014, specifically comparing the percentages of home owners and renters.

Overall, the period shows a clear and opposing trend for the two groups. There was a significant decline in the proportion of young adults owning their own home, while the percentage of those renting increased substantially.

In 2004, home ownership was the more popular option, with nearly 60% of people in this age group owning their house. However, this figure fell steadily throughout the decade, reaching a low of approximately 37% by 2014.

In contrast, only about 21% of 25-34 year olds were renters at the beginning of the period. This percentage saw a consistent rise, more than doubling to roughly 48% in 2014. A significant crossover occurred in 2012, when renting became more common than home ownership for this demographic for the first time.
