Activity: Data Visualization and Chart Analysis

Topic: Global Social Media Usage

Data Source and Topic Selection:
For this activity, the chosen topic is the global popularity of social media platforms. Data was referenced from industry reports by DataReportal and Statista for January 2024. The metric selected for comparison is the number of monthly active users (MAUs), which is a standard measure of a platform's reach and engagement.

Chart Elaboration and Design:
A vertical bar chart was created to visually represent and compare the user numbers of the top platforms. 

**Chart Title:** Most Popular Social Media Platforms Worldwide by Active Users (January 2024)

**Y-Axis Label:** Number of Active Users (in Billions)

**X-Axis Label:** Social Media Platform

**Data Points Represented:**
- Facebook: 3.05 billion
- YouTube: 2.78 billion
- WhatsApp: 2.00 billion
- Instagram: 2.00 billion
- WeChat: 1.34 billion
- TikTok: 1.22 billion

**Formal Edits Applied:**
- A clear and descriptive title was added.
- Both axes were explicitly labeled to define the data and categories.
- The unit of measurement (billions) was clearly stated on the Y-axis.
- A source attribution was included at the bottom of the chart for academic integrity.
- The bars were arranged in descending order to facilitate easy comparison.
- Data labels were placed on top of each bar to provide precise values.

**Source:** DataReportal, Statista (2024).


Chart Description and Analysis:

**1. Introduction**
The bar chart, titled “Most Popular Social Media Platforms Worldwide by Active Users (January 2024),” illustrates the comparative ranking of the world's leading social networks. The data, presented in billions of users, provides a clear snapshot of the global social media landscape at the beginning of 2024.

**2. Key Features**
The most prominent feature of the chart is Facebook's clear dominance, standing as the only platform to surpass three billion monthly active users (3.05 billion). Its closest competitor, YouTube, holds a strong second position with 2.78 billion users. Following them is a tie for third place between two Meta-owned platforms, WhatsApp and Instagram, both registering 2.00 billion users. This highlights the significant market control held by Meta. A considerable gap separates these top four from the next tier, which includes WeChat (1.34 billion) and TikTok (1.22 billion).

**3. Conclusion**
In summary, the chart effectively communicates the immense scale of the top social media platforms and the established hierarchy within the market. Meta's portfolio of apps (Facebook, WhatsApp, Instagram) collectively engages a vast majority of global users. While newer platforms like TikTok show significant user numbers, they still trail the established leaders. The visualization underscores the consolidated nature of the social media industry, with a few key players commanding the attention of billions worldwide.
